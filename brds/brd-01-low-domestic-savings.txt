BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (BRD)

1. Change Overview
Title: Revision of Interest Rate Slabs on Domestic Recurring Deposit Scheme
Change Type: Change to Existing Product
Requested By: Business Owner
Business Unit: Retail Banking
Summary: Treasury has proposed a 10 to 25 basis-point revision across the
interest rate slabs of the bank's existing domestic Recurring Deposit
product (tenures 12 to 60 months). The change is a pure pricing
parameter update. No customer onboarding change, no channel change, no
geography change, no third-party change. Aligns with RBI's deregulated
interest rate regime for resident bank deposits (Master Circular
DBR.No.FSD.BC.18/05.05.010/2024-25 dated 1 July 2024).

2. Product Description
Product Name: Domestic Recurring Deposit Scheme (RD)
Product Category: Deposit Products
Description: Existing monthly-installment Recurring Deposit for resident
Indian salaried and self-employed customers. Proposal is to revise the
slab-wise interest rates for tenures 12, 24, 36, 48 and 60 months.
Senior citizen rates are recalibrated in line with the revised base
slab. No change to premature withdrawal rules, no change to loan
against RD, no change to nomination rules.
Transaction Type: Domestic deposit (monthly standing instruction debit)
Currency: INR
Countries Supported: India
Per-Transaction Limit: 200000 (per installment)
Expected Monthly Volume: 8500
New Product Flag: No
