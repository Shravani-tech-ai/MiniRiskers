BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (BRD)

1. Change Overview
Title: Launch of Mobile-First Personal Loan on the Account Aggregator Framework
Change Type: New Product
Requested By: Business Owner
Business Unit: Digital Lending
Summary: Launch of a fully digital, mobile-first personal loan product
for salaried and self-employed professionals who hold an active savings
or salary account with the bank. Loan origination, underwriting, and
disbursement run entirely on the mobile banking app. Underwriting uses
the bank's own bureau aggregator plus the RBI Account Aggregator (AA)
framework (Master Direction on NBFC AA, 2016, updated 2024) for consent-
based financial data fetch. Disbursement and EMI collection stay in-
house; no external Loan Service Provider (LSP) is engaged. This BRD is
being raised in response to RBI's Digital Lending Guidelines
(DBR.No.FSD.BC.45/05.10.001/2022-23 dated 10 August 2022, updated via
circular DBR.No.FSD.BC.102/05.10.001/2023-24 dated 14 December 2023)
which require every digital lending partnership to be assessed.

or salary account with the bank.
