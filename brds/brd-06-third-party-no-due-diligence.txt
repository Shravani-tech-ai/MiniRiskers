BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (BRD)

1. Change Overview
Title: Onboarding of New Cross-Border Card Processor (DD Pending)
Change Type: New Product
Requested By: Business Owner
Business Unit: Cards and Merchant Services
Summary: The Cards business has finalised a new vendor, CardStream
Processing Ireland Ltd, to power the upcoming co-branded travel card
product. The commercial contract is currently in legal review and the
risk and information-security due diligence is incomplete. The Cards
business is requesting a one-time exception to begin a controlled
pilot (2,000 cards, INR 50 lakh aggregate monthly spend limit) while
DD finishes in the next 60 to 90 days. This BRD is being raised in
the context of RBI Master Direction on Outsourcing of IT Services
(DBR.No.FSD.BC.83/31.01.002/2023-24 dated 10 April 2024, successor
to the 2017 direction) which requires that material outsourcing
arrangements complete risk and security due diligence BEFORE
processing begins.

8. Additional Notes
This BRD is designed to trip RISK-FLOOR RULE #2 in the risk engine:
"third-party processing without completed vendor due diligence."
Due Diligence Completed = No, Contract Completed = No, Audit Rights =
No, BCP = No. All four key RBI outsourcing controls are missing. The
Cards business's argument is that this is a limited pilot, but RBI's
April 2024 Master Direction is explicit that material outsourcing
arrangements must complete DD BEFORE going live. Verify that the
residual rating is forced to CRITICAL regardless of how strong the
bank's own controls are, and that the AI recommendation explicitly
flags the regulatory breach under RBI's IT Outsourcing direction.
Regulatory evidence should surface RBI Master Direction on Outsourcing
of IT Services 2023-24, the RBI Master Direction on IT Governance Risk
and Controls Assurance Practices 2023, and PCI-DSS 4.0 obligations.
This BRD is a candidate for committee rejection on grounds of
unmitigated regulatory exposure.
