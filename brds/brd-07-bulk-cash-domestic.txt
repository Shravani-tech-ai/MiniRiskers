BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (BRD)

1. Change Overview
Title: Cash-Intensive Bullion and Jeweller Trade Settlement Account
Change Type: New Product
Requested By: Business Owner
Business Unit: Wholesale Banking - Trade and Settlement
Summary: New product offering specialised current accounts plus a
structured cash deposit and aggregation service to jewellers, bullion
dealers, and precious-metals retailers. Account holders deposit bulk
cash at designated branches (typically in bullion market clusters such
as Mumbai's Zaveri Bazaar, Chennai's Mannady, Jaipur's Johari Bazaar,
and Delhi's Chandni Chowk). Cash is counted by the bank's back-office
cash processing unit, aggregated, and either credited to the
customer's current account on a T+0 basis or used to settle bullion
purchases from empanelled suppliers. Daily cash handling per
designated branch is expected to be INR 25 to 75 crore.

8. Additional Notes
This BRD is a HIGH-risk scenario driven by the cash-intensive
transaction profile and the high-risk customer segment (jewellery and
bullion industry). It is fully domestic: no cross-border, no high-risk
jurisdiction, no sanctions exposure, and the CIT vendor has full DD so
risk-floor rule #2 must NOT fire. Use this BRD to confirm that the
risk engine correctly elevates the Transaction risk category on the
basis of cash_involved = true, Transaction Velocity = 4, and Round
Amount Risk = Yes, even when geography is fully domestic. Expected
band: HIGH, landing just below the CRITICAL thresholds. Regulatory
evidence should surface PMLA 2002 record-keeping obligations, the
PML Maintenance of Records Rules 2005 (Rule 3 - CTR at INR 10 lakh),
the FIU-IND master guidance on Cash Balance Sheet Reporting (CBSR)
2023, and the RBI Master Direction on KYC section on high-risk
customer categories. Verify that the residual risk does NOT cross into
CRITICAL despite the cash intensity, because no risk-floor rule
applies.
