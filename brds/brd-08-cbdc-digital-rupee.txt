BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (BRD)

1. Change Overview
Title: Launch of CBDC (Digital Rupee / e₹-R) Merchant Acquiring and P2P Service
Change Type: New Product
Requested By: Business Owner
Business Unit: Digital Payments and CBDC
Summary: Launch of customer and merchant acceptance for the RBI's
Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) — the Digital Rupee (e₹-R) — in
the bank's mobile banking app. This is being raised in response to RBI's
expansion of the e₹-R retail pilot since 1 December 2022 and the
programmability pilots launched with the Indian Banks' Association in
2024. The pilot is currently being expanded to include merchant
acquiring, P2P transfers, and integration with the Unified Payments
Interface (UPI) as an interoperable token-based instrument. The bank is
one of the 15 scheduled commercial banks actively participating in
the e₹-R retail pilot.

2. Product Description
Product Name: Digital Rupee (e₹-R) Wallet and Merchant Acquiring
Product Category: CBDC / Digital Payments
Description: Tokenised Digital Rupee wallet for retail customers and a
merchant acquiring solution for QR-based e₹-R acceptance. Backed by
RBI-issued digital tokens settled on the bank's reserve balance with
RBI. Programmability use cases include time-bound tokens (e₹-R-T) for
subsidy disbursement and location-bound tokens (e₹-R-L) for
geo-restricted tourism spending. Tokens are issued, redeemed, and
destroyed only through the RBI-licensed Digital Rupee platform.
Transaction Type: Tokenised CBDC transfer (P2P and P2M)
Currency: INR (tokenised)
Countries Supported: India only (RBI has not yet enabled cross-border
CBDC interoperability at retail level)
Per-Transaction Limit: 100000 (per RBI pilot cap; subject to revision)
Expected Monthly Volume: 50000
New Product Flag: Yes
