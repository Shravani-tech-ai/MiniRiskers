BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (BRD)

1. Change Overview
Title: Co-Lending Arrangement with FinTech NBFC for MSME Digital Loans
Change Type: New Product
Requested By: Business Owner
Business Unit: MSME Lending and FinTech Partnerships
Summary: Enter into a co-lending arrangement with CashCredit FinTech
NBFC Ltd (a RBI-registered NBFC) to originate and service unsecured
small-ticket working-capital loans to MSME borrowers via the FinTech's
mobile app and loan management system. Disbursement is split 80/20
between the bank (80%) and the FinTech NBFC (20%), per the revised
RBI Co-Lending Guidelines (DBR.No.BP.BC.45/21.04.048/2018-19 dated 4
December 2018, revised 25 November 2020, with the latest 80/20
modification communicated via circular
DBR.No.BP.BC.86/21.04.048/2020-21 dated 5 November 2021). The bank's
role under RBI's Digital Lending Guidelines (10 August 2022) is to
ensure that the FinTech NBFC's digital lending stack, recovery
practices, and disclosure standards are at par with the bank's own.

2. Product Description
Product Name: MSME Digi-Loan Co-Lending Product
Product Category: MSME Lending / Co-Lending
Description: Unsecured working-capital loan for MSME borrowers, ticket
size INR 50,000 to INR 25 lakh, tenures 6 to 36 months. Origination
happens entirely on the FinTech NBFC's app (sanctioned by the bank
under the Digital Lending Guidelines); underwriting combines the
FinTech's cash-flow underwriting model with the bank's bureau and
Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE)
data. Disbursement is into the MSME's current account; EMI is debited
from the same. Default handling follows the bank's standard MSME
recovery playbook.
Transaction Type: Domestic MSME loan disbursement and EMI collection
Currency: INR
Countries Supported: India
Per-Transaction Limit: 2500000
Expected Monthly Volume: 4000
New Product Flag: Yes

8. Additional Notes
This BRD represents one of the topmost real-time risk areas for
Indian banks in 2025-2026. RBI has been actively flagging concerns
about digital lending apps and co-lending arrangements, and ED / FIU
have taken action against multiple FinTech NBFCs for PMLA and
disclosure breaches. Use this BRD to confirm the risk engine
correctly classifies the third-party relationship as Material
Outsourcing and pulls in the relevant RBI Digital Lending Guidelines,
the 2021 co-lending 80/20 modification, and the CGTMSE framework as
regulatory evidence. Expected band: MEDIUM to HIGH — driven by New
Product, Third-Party Channel, and Digital Lending App exposure.
Residual rating should improve meaningfully because the vendor has
full DD, the contract is complete, and audit rights plus BCP are in
place. This BRD is the contrast to brd-06 (where DD is pending) —
same product shape, but the controls are properly executed.
