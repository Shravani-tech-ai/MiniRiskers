BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (BRD)

1. Change Overview
Title: Extension of Outbound Remittance Corridors to Iran and Myanmar
Change Type: Change to Existing Product
Requested By: Business Owner
Business Unit: Payments and Remittances
Summary: Proposal to extend the bank's existing outbound LRS remittance
product to two new corridors: Iran (Tehran) and Myanmar (Yangon). Both
jurisdictions are currently on the FATF list of high-risk jurisdictions
subject to a call for action (Iran, DPRK, Myanmar) or grey list (as of
the FATF plenary most recent to this BRD). Treasury expects the
combined Iran plus Myanmar corridor to drive the largest share of
monthly outbound volume within six months of launch, primarily from
the bank's existing business correspondent partnerships.

8. Additional Notes
This BRD is designed to trip RISK-FLOOR RULE #1 in the risk engine:
"cross-border activity involving high-risk jurisdiction AND high
transaction velocity." Iran and Myanmar are both on the FATF high-risk
list as of 2025-2026. Velocity is 4 and daily volume is 1300. Sanctions
exposure is also flagged because Iran is under OFAC sanctions. Verify
that the residual rating is forced to CRITICAL even if the calculated
inherent score (without the floor rule) might otherwise land in HIGH.
Regulatory evidence should surface the RBI Master Direction on KYC
(section on high-risk jurisdictions), the FATF high-risk jurisdiction
list, OFAC sanctions guidance for Iran, and the PMLA obligations on
enhanced due diligence for high-risk corridors. Note: in production,
this BRD should NOT be approved until legal and compliance have signed
off on the specific Iran and Myanmar payout paths.
