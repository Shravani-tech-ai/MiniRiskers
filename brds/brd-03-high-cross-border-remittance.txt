BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (BRD)

1. Change Overview
Title: Launch of Digital Outbound International Remittance Service under LRS
Change Type: New Product
Requested By: Business Owner
Business Unit: Payments and Remittances
Summary: Launch of a new digital outbound international remittance
product allowing resident Indian retail customers to send money
abroad under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS) limit of USD
250,000 per financial year per individual (per FEMA Notification
FEMA.275/2013-RB and subsequent amendments). Settlement happens via a
third-party cross-border payment processor. Customer journey is
mobile-first with KYC under the RBI Master Direction on KYC
(DBR.AML.BC.81/14.01.001/2014-15 dated 25 February 2015, last updated
4 January 2024).

2. Product Description
Product Name: Digital International Remittance (LRS)
Product Category: Cross-Border Payments
Description: Outbound retail remittance under LRS. Customers fund the
remittance from their savings or current account with the bank.
Beneficiaries can receive cash pickup or bank account credit. Mobile
app shows live FX rates and TCS (Tax Collected at Source) computation
under Section 206C of the Income Tax Act (10% above INR 7 lakh per
financial year, 20% above INR 10 lakh, with educational and health
exemptions).
Transaction Type: Cross-border remittance under LRS
Currency: INR, USD, AED, GBP, EUR, SGD, PHP
Countries Supported: India plus UAE, USA, UK, EU, Singapore, Philippines
Per-Transaction Limit: 500000 (INR equivalent; well within LRS cap)
Expected Monthly Volume: 22000
New Product Flag: Yes

Cross-border retail remittance under LRS with a regulated Singapore-
based settlement partner. No high-risk jurisdiction, no sanctions
exposure, vendor DD is fully complete. This BRD should land in HIGH on
the strength of cross-border + new product + third-party channel +
