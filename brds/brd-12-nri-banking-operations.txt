BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (BRD)

1. Change Overview
Title: NRI Banking Suite (NRO / NRE / FCNR) Operational Enhancement
Change Type: Change to Existing Product
Requested By: Business Owner
Business Unit: NRI Banking and Remittances
Summary: Refresh of the bank's NRI Banking suite to consolidate and
modernise operations across NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary), NRE (Non-
Resident External), and FCNR (Foreign Currency Non-Resident) account
categories. Proposal is to add a unified mobile-app view across the
three account types, enable SWIFT-based inward remittance tracking
from major Gulf and US corridors, and roll out a Tier-1 KYC refresh
programme for the top 10% of NRI customers by balance. The suite
serves the bank's 32 lakh NRI customer base, primarily resident in
the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, the United
States, the United Kingdom, Canada, and Singapore. This BRD is being
raised in the context of RBI's liberalised NRI deposit framework
(Master Direction on Interest Rates on NRI Deposits and other
directions updated 5 December 2023) and the increasing compliance
expectations on inward remittance source-of-funds documentation.

2. Product Description
Product Name: NRI Banking Suite (NRO / NRE / FCNR)
Product Category: NRI Banking / Cross-Border Accounts
Description: The NRI Banking suite bundles three account categories
into a single mobile experience for the bank's NRI customers:
NRO (rupee account for income earned in India, fully repatriable
for current income), NRE (rupee account for foreign currency
inflows, principal and interest fully repatriable), and FCNR
(foreign currency term deposit in USD, GBP, EUR, JPY, AUD, CAD,
SGD, HKD, CHF). The suite adds a unified SWIFT-based inward
remittance tracker and a Tier-1 KYC refresh for high-balance NRI
customers.
Transaction Type: Inbound cross-border remittance, FCNR deposit
renewals, NRO/NRE repatriation
Currency: INR, USD, AED, GBP, EUR, SAR, KWD, CAD, SGD, AUD, JPY,
CHF, HKD
Countries Supported: India plus UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar,
Bahrain, Oman, USA, UK, Canada, Singapore
Per-Transaction Limit: 50000000 (FCNR maximum per customer per bank
under FEMA)
Expected Monthly Volume: 350000
New Product Flag: No

8. Additional Notes
This BRD represents the largest cross-border customer-facing flow
the bank handles: 32 lakh NRI customers driving 3.5 lakh transactions
per month. The risk profile is dominated by customer-segment risk
(NRI with PEP exposure and high-balance concentration) rather than
by jurisdiction risk. Expected band: HIGH. The risk engine should
elevate the Customer and Transaction risk dimensions significantly
because of the high transaction velocity (3), large per-transaction
limits (up to INR 5 crore), and PEP exposure. The risk engine should
NOT trigger any risk-floor rule because no jurisdiction is high-risk
and there is no third-party issue. Regulatory evidence should surface
RBI's NRI Master Direction, FEMA 1999 deposit framework, PMLA 2002
obligations on inward remittances (source-of-funds documentation), and
FATF guidance on remittance corridors.
