BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (BRD)

1. Change Overview
Title: Establishment of Crude Oil Import Letter of Credit Facility
Change Type: New Product
Requested By: Business Owner
Business Unit: Trade Finance and Wholesale Banking
Summary: Open a revolving Import Letter of Credit (LC) facility for the
bank's flagship Indian PSU oil marketing client for the import of
crude oil from a basket of suppliers across the Middle East, Africa,
Latin America, and Russia. Settlement happens in USD via the bank's
existing USD nostro accounts with JPMorgan Chase, New York and
Standard Chartered Bank, London. This BRD is being raised in the
context of post-2022 sanctions on Russian crude (OFAC, EU, and UK
sanctions regimes; G7 price cap on Russian crude at USD 60/barrel
since December 2022) and India's continued discounted purchases of
Russian crude via the Banking and Finance Brief on Russia-related
sanctions issued by the Ministry of External Affairs and the Reserve
Bank's advisory dated 14 March 2022 and subsequent updates.

2. Product Description
Product Name: Crude Oil Import LC Revolving Facility
Product Category: Trade Finance / Import LC
Description: Revolving USD-denominated Import Letter of Credit in
favour of multiple crude oil suppliers (mostly National Oil Companies
and IOC trading desks) for the import of crude oil cargoes into
Indian refineries (Sikka, Mundra, Paradip, and Chennai Petroleum
Corporation). Each LC covers a single cargo valued between USD 30
million and USD 120 million. Settlement is on a sight basis against
Bill of Lading, Commercial Invoice, Certificate of Origin, SGS
quality certificate, and Q88 ship details. Insurance is arranged
separately through the buyer's open policy.
Transaction Type: Cross-border trade finance (Import LC and USD
settlement)
Currency: USD (primary), with INR accounting leg
Countries Supported: India plus UAE, Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Kuwait,
Nigeria, Angola, Brazil, Russia, USA (for certain term contracts)
Per-Transaction Limit: 120000000 USD (single cargo ceiling)
Expected Monthly Volume: 25
New Product Flag: Yes

8. Additional Notes
This BRD represents one of the most consequential international
transaction categories for any major Indian bank. Crude oil imports
are the single largest line item in India's import bill and account
for the lion's share of USD nostro flows. The risk dimension is
dominated by:
- High transaction value (USD 30M to 120M per cargo)
- Russia exposure (sanctions and price cap compliance)
- Middle East and African supplier jurisdictions (some tier-2 concerns)
- USD correspondent settlement (OFAC obligations on the bank's NY
  correspondent)
- PSU customer with PEP exposure on the board

Expected band: HIGH for the standard facility with non-sanctioned
suppliers; CRITICAL when individual cargoes are sourced from Russia
(forces compliance overlay on top of standard risk score). Use this
BRD to confirm that the risk engine correctly surfaces the FATF high-
risk flag (Russia) and the Sanctions Exposure flag independently, and
that regulatory evidence pulls in the RBI Russia advisory of March
2022, OFAC Russia-related FAQs, and the G7 price cap guidance.
