BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (BRD)

1. Change Overview
Title: Education Corridor Remittance Service (LRS - Education Purpose)
Change Type: New Product
Requested By: Business Owner
Business Unit: NRI Remittances and Education Lending
Summary: Launch of a dedicated cross-border remittance product for the
Education Purpose code under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS).
The product is being raised in the context of the 1.5 million+
Indian students studying abroad (primarily in the USA, UK, Canada,
Australia, Ireland, Germany, and New Zealand) and the Education
Ministry's Project Abroad 2024 announcement which expects this number
to grow 15-20% YoY through 2027. Education remittances are projected
to grow from approximately USD 5 billion in FY24 to over USD 8
billion by FY27. The product bundles tuition fee transfers, living
expense remittances, and a co-lent education loan to cover the gap
between available funds and total cost of attendance.

2. Product Description
Product Name: EduPay Education Corridor Remittance
Product Category: Cross-Border Remittances / Education Lending
Description: Multi-purpose LRS remittance product for Indian residents
sending funds abroad for a dependent's education. Three flows are
bundled: (a) tuition fee transfer directly to the foreign university
under Form A2 with purpose code 'Education' (P0001) and TCS at 0.5%
above INR 7 lakh per financial year per LRS (lower than the general
20% TCS for non-education LRS); (b) living expense remittance to the
student's own foreign bank account; (c) co-lent education loan to
cover the funding gap, in tie-up with the bank's NBFC arm, capped at
INR 1.5 crore per student per program.
Transaction Type: Outbound LRS remittance (Education purpose) plus
domestic education loan disbursement
Currency: INR, USD, GBP, AUD, CAD, EUR, NZD
Countries Supported: India plus USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Ireland,
Germany, New Zealand, Singapore
Per-Transaction Limit: 5000000 (per transaction; cumulative subject to
LRS USD 250,000 per FY per remitter)
Expected Monthly Volume: 12000
New Product Flag: Yes

8. Additional Notes
This BRD represents one of the fastest-growing cross-border retail
flows in India. Education remittance is a strategically important
LRS purpose code because it carries a lower TCS rate (0.5% above
INR 7L vs 20% general LRS above INR 10L), making it attractive to
customers. The risk profile is dominated by the cross-border nature
of the flow and the third-party payment aggregator. The vendor is
rated Medium criticality because it is a tactical payment utility,
not a core service vendor. Expected band: MEDIUM to HIGH. Use this
BRD to confirm that the risk engine correctly classifies a
purpose-coded LRS flow (Education) at MEDIUM, not HIGH, when the
underlying vendor and jurisdictions are clean. Regulatory evidence
should surface RBI's LRS Master Direction, Form A2 instructions,
Section 206C of the Income Tax Act on Education TCS exemption up to
INR 7L, FEMA 1999, and the Education Ministry's Project Abroad 2024
guidance.
