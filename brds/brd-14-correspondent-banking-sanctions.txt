BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (BRD)

1. Change Overview
Title: USD Correspondent Banking Relationship Onboarding (OFAC-Compliant)
Change Type: New Product
Requested By: Business Owner
Business Unit: Correspondent Banking and Financial Institutions
Summary: Onboard a new USD correspondent banking relationship with a
mid-sized US regional bank to expand the bank's USD clearing capacity
for trade finance, oil import LC settlement, and FCNR deposit
servicing. The new correspondent brings an additional nostro account
in New York, supplementing the bank's existing JPMorgan Chase and
Standard Chartered London relationships. This BRD is being raised
in the context of OFAC's heightened scrutiny of USD clearing for
Indian banks (OFAC's enforcement actions against multiple
international banks in 2023-2024 over Russia and Iran-related
sanctions screening failures) and the bank's own obligation under
the USA PATRIOT Act Section 311 to maintain a robust Correspondent
Account Due Diligence programme. Also covers the FATF Recommendation
13 (Correspondent Banking) and FATF Recommendation 16 (Wire Transfer
Information) compliance obligations.

8. Additional Notes
This BRD represents the topmost risk area in international banking
in 2025: USD correspondent banking under OFAC scrutiny. India's RBI
Master Direction on KYC, the Wolfsberg Group Correspondent Banking
DDQ, and the USA PATRIOT Act Section 311 collectively require that
Indian banks perform rigorous DD on their USD correspondents. The
risk dimension is dominated by:
- USD clearing reach (subject to OFAC jurisdiction exposure for any
  USD payment worldwide)
- FATF Recommendation 13 obligations
- USA PATRIOT Act Section 311 obligations on the correspondent's own
  AML programme
- High transaction velocity and value (4500 transactions per month,
  average USD 5M per transaction)

Expected band: HIGH, driven primarily by transaction velocity and
cross-border reach. The risk engine should NOT trigger any
risk-floor rule because the customer (the new correspondent) is a
regulated US bank with full DD, but Sanctions Exposure should be
flagged at the Geography level because USD clearing inherently
carries OFAC exposure. Regulatory evidence should surface the USA
PATRIOT Act Section 311, the Wolfsberg Group Correspondent Banking
DDQ, FATF Recommendations 13 and 16, RBI's Master Direction on KYC
Section on Correspondent Banking, and OFAC's Sanctions Compliance
Guidance for the Financial Services Sector (2014, updated 2023).
