# Business Owner Guide

> **Your job in one sentence:** describe the new product or change you want to launch, submit it for a financial crime risk check, follow its progress, and respond if the committee asks for more.

You are the **starting point** of every request. Nothing reaches the Risk Analyst or the Committee until you **submit** it.

Shared topics (signing in, notifications, Settings, Track your Progress) are in the [index](README.md).

## 1. What you can and cannot do

| ✅ You can | ❌ You cannot |
|---|---|
| Create new change requests | See other Business Owners' requests |
| Upload a BRD and let AI pre-fill the form | Edit a request after submitting it (unless it is returned to you) |
| Fill in and save the six intake sections | See risk scores before the analyst has reviewed them |
| Submit the request for review | Open the **Analyst** stage (the analyst's internal notes) |
| See the analyst's final rating and suggestions | See the committee's discussion workspace |
| See the committee's decision and rationale | See the AI draft before the committee decides |
| Submit evidence for approval conditions | Open the Methodology, Regulations or Admin pages |
| Rate the process after it closes | Export audit packs |

**Why these limits?** Two reasons:

- **Independence:** the analyst and committee must assess your request without pressure, so their working notes stay private until a decision is made.
- **A reliable record:** once you submit, your inputs are frozen so everyone is judging the same facts.

### Risk overview bar

## 4. Creating a new request (3 steps)

Open it with **Requests → New request**. The page is titled **"New change request"**.

### Step 1: Change details

#### Optional: "Start from a BRD"

A BRD (Business Requirements Document) is the document your team already writes to describe the change.

| Button | What it does | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Sample BRD** | Downloads `MiniRiskers-sample-BRD.pdf` | Shows the format the system reads best. Lines like `Title: …` and `Business Unit: …` are picked up automatically |
| **Upload BRD** | Reads your file (.pdf, .docx, .txt or .md) and pre-fills this form | Saves typing. The file is attached to the request when you create it |
| **Replace BRD** | Swaps in a different file | — |
| 🗑 (trash) | Removes the BRD | — |

After uploading you'll see one of:

- *"Pre-filled: {fields}. Review the values before continuing."*
- *"No request details were recognised; fill the form manually. The BRD will still be attached."*

If the BRD says something that doesn't match a dropdown option, an amber hint appears, e.g. *BRD says "Retail & SME" — choose the closest option.*

> Image-only (scanned) PDFs can't be read. Use a PDF with selectable text, a DOCX, or a TXT file.

#### "Change details" card

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|:-:|---|
| Request title | ✅ | e.g. "Digital international remittance launch" |
| Description | ✅ | What changes, which channels and countries, and the go-live context |
| Change type | ✅ | New product · Product change · Process change · Vendor change · Geography change · Customer segment |

Click **Continue**.

### Step 2: Classification

| Field | Required | Options |
|---|:-:|---|
| Product type | ✅ | Free text, stored in UPPER_CASE (e.g. `DIGITAL_REMITTANCE`) |
| Business unit | ✅ | Retail Banking · Commercial Banking · Payments · Wealth Management |
| Customer segment | ✅ | Retail Customers · Business Customers · Retail and Business Customers · High Net Worth Customers |

Click **Continue**.

### Step 3: Review

- **Requested by** is filled in automatically from your account and cannot be changed. This is how the system knows the request is **yours**.
- **Intake summary** lists everything you entered, plus the BRD's status.

Click **Create & open assessment**.

> **Create & open assessment** saves the request as a **Draft**. It does **not** send it to the analyst yet; you do that later from the Preview tab ([section 6](#6-submitting-for-risk-analyst-review)).
>
> If you chose a BRD but it couldn't be attached, the request page shows *"The request was created, but the BRD could not be attached…"*. Upload it again in the Intake method panel.

**What happens:**

1. The request gets a number like `CR-2026-007`. Its status is **Draft** and its stage is **Request**.
2. Your BRD (if any) is attached.
3. You get a notification: *"Request CR-… created"*.
4. An audit event `CREATED_CHANGE_REQUEST` is recorded.
5. You land on the request's **Assessment page**.

> **What others see now:** still nothing. The draft is visible only to you and Admins.

Use **Cancel** (step 1) or **Back** (steps 2–3) to leave or go back.

## 5. Filling in the intake on the Assessment page

The request page has a header with the request number, a status pill, the title and the description. While the request is a Draft, you see two columns:

```text
┌─────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Intake method      │  Assessment inputs                              │
│  (BRD upload /      │  [Product][Customer][Geography][Transaction]    │
│   Manual entry)     │  [Channel][Vendor][Preview]                     │
└─────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Left side: Intake method panel

The header shows **Completeness: {n}%**, the share of the 11 required fields that are saved.

| Button | What it does | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **BRD upload / Manual entry** (toggle) | Shows or hides the BRD tools | Choose AI-assisted or fully manual entry |
| **Upload BRD** | Uploads a file (.pdf/.docx/.txt/.md) and shows how much text was read | The AI needs readable text to work |
| **Extract fields** | AI (Gemini), plus rule-based parsing, reads the BRD and proposes values for **all six sections** | Saves a lot of typing on long BRDs. Enabled only once a BRD with text is uploaded |
| **Download** | Downloads the uploaded BRD | Check exactly which file is attached |
| **Remove** | Detaches the BRD from the request | The file is not deleted: it stays in the request's document history, which the analyst, committee and admin can see. Uploading a new BRD likewise keeps the old one as an earlier version |
| **Sample BRD (PDF)** | Downloads the template | — |

**After Extract**, a **"Review extracted values"** box appears:

- It says either *"Combined AI extraction and rule-based field mapping."* or, if the AI was unavailable, *"AI was unavailable; rule-based parsing was used. Review fields carefully."*
- A table compares **Field / Extracted / Saved**.
- A warning lists required fields still missing.
- An **Apply & save to forms** button saves the extracted values into the intake and fills the tabs on the right.

> **Review the table before you click.** AI can make mistakes, so this click is your human check (review gate G1 in the governance docs). Nothing extracted is saved until you press it.
>
> After saving, each completed tab shows **✓ Saved**. Fill in anything still missing, and you can still **Edit** any tab.
>
> If a required value (e.g. product name) is missing, the server can't save the extraction. You'll see an error, and the values are filled into the tabs for you to finish and **Save** section by section.

You also get a notification, *"BRD extracted for CR-…"*.

### Right side: the six intake tabs

The tabs must be completed **in order**. A tab stays 🔒 locked (*"Save the previous sections first"*) until all earlier tabs are saved.

| Tab | Card title | Required fields (*) | Other useful fields |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Product** | Product Information | Product Name, Transaction Type | Category, Description, Currency, Transaction Limit, Expected Volume, Frequency, Supported Countries; ☐ Digital / Branch / Agent channel, ☐ Cross Border, ☐ Cash Involved |
| **Customer** | Customer Profile | Customer Type (Individual / Business / Individual & Business), Onboarding Method | PEP and high-risk customer flags, population size, etc. |
| **Geography** | Geography | Country, Transaction Country, Beneficiary Country | Geographic Exposure (Domestic / Cross Border), ☐ High-Risk Jurisdiction, ☐ Sanctions Exposure |
| **Transaction** | Transaction Profile | Transaction Type | Amounts, volumes, number of countries, velocity; ☐ Cash, ☐ Cross-Border, ☐ Round Amount Pattern, ☐ Rapid Movement Possible |
| **Channel** | Channel Information | Channel Type (Digital / Branch / Agent / API / Third Party) | 7 channel checkboxes |
| **Vendor** | Vendor / Third-Party Information | Vendor Name, Vendor Type | Criticality (Low–Critical), risk and governance checkboxes |
| **Preview** | Review & Submit | — | A read-only summary of all six sections, plus the Submit button |

**Why these fields matter:** they become the system's **risk factors**. For example:

- ☐ Cross Border, high velocity and ☐ Rapid Movement together trigger the *Cross-border velocity concentration* rule, which keeps the residual risk at 60 or above.
- ☐ Sanctions Exposure triggers a floor of 65.

Answer accurately. Under-reporting will be caught later and may get your request **returned**.

### Buttons at the bottom of each tab

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Save {Section}** (e.g. "Save Product") | Saves this section to the server. Shows *"Saving..."* while it works, then a **✓ Saved** pill appears |
| **Edit** | Re-opens a saved section for changes |
| **Save changes** | Saves after editing |
| **Cancel** | Throws away unsaved edits and reloads the saved values |
| **Previous: {tab}** / **Next: {tab}** | Moves between tabs. **Next** stays disabled (*"Save {label} to continue"*) until you save |

If a required field is empty: *"Please fill in the required fields: …"*.

### The footer hint

The hint at the bottom of the page tells you what to do next:

- *"Required fields are incomplete. Complete and save each section, then use the Preview tab to submit…"*
- *"All required fields are complete. Open the Preview tab above to review the intake and submit it…"*

> **What others see now:** still nothing. Analysts can't see the request until you submit.

## 6. Submitting for Risk Analyst review

1. Open the **Preview** tab ("Review & Submit"). Check every section; empty values show as "—".
2. Click **Submit for Risk Analyst review**.
   - It stays disabled until all 11 required fields are saved. The helper text lists what is missing.
3. A confirmation appears: **"Submit this change request?"** — *"Once submitted, it will move into the Risk Analyst queue for review. Do you really want to submit?"*. Click **Yes, submit** (or **Cancel**).
4. Result: **"Submitted successfully"** — *"Change request submitted. It is now visible in the Risk Analyst queue."*

**Why it matters:** this is the hand-off point. It freezes your inputs and **starts the 48-hour SLA clock**.

**What happens in the system:**

| Effect | Detail |
|---|---|
| Status | Draft → **Submitted** |
| Your inputs | 🔒 **Locked.** A green banner reads *"Submitted for Risk Analyst review on {date}. Inputs are now locked."* The Intake panel disappears and the tabs become read-only |
| SLA | The clock starts (target 48h) |
| Audit | `CHANGE_REQUEST_SUBMITTED` |
| You receive | *"Request CR-… submitted for risk review"* |

> **What others see now:**
>
> | Role | What they see |
> |---|---|
> | **Risk Analyst** | A notification, *"New request CR-… submitted — ready for risk assessment"*. The request appears in their dashboard under **New (not yet assessed)** with a **Start risk assessment** button |
> | **Risk Committee** | Nothing yet. The committee only sees requests that reach committee review |
> | **Admin** | The same notification as analysts, if no specific analyst is assigned |

## 7. Following your request after submission

Open the request (Dashboard → **View status**) and use the **← / →** buttons in the footer to move between views:

**← Request details** · **Risk assessment** · **Committee review** · **Outcome**

> The **Analyst review** view is **skipped for you** and shown as *"Not available for your role"* in the stepper. It holds the analyst's working notes.

### What you see at each moment

| Your request is… | Request view | Risk assessment view | Committee view |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Submitted, analyst hasn't started** | Read-only intake. Footer: *"Awaiting Risk Analyst. This request is in the Risk Analyst queue…"* | *"Risk results are under analyst review — The Risk Analyst has not run the risk assessment yet."* | not reachable yet |
| **Analyst ran the risk assessment** | read-only | *"Risk results are under analyst review — The risk assessment has been calculated. It will appear here once the Risk Analyst has reviewed it…"* (scores are still **hidden** from you) | not reachable yet |
| **Analyst submitted their review** | read-only | **Unlocked:** Risk Analyst outcome card, Risk Overview, Similar past cases, Regulatory evidence | *"Awaiting committee decision — This request is with the Risk Committee (since …). Committee deliberations are visible to committee members only…"* |
| **Committee decided** | read-only | as above, **plus the AI assessment** | *"Committee decision recorded"* and a **View outcome →** button |

**Why the scores are hidden at first:** the system's number is only a starting point. You see the result once a qualified analyst has confirmed or adjusted it, so you never act on an unreviewed score.

### The "Risk Analyst outcome" card

- *"The analyst accepted the system-calculated rating."* **or** *"The analyst adjusted the rating from HIGH to MEDIUM."*, followed by *"Reviewed by {name}."*
- **Final rating** pill.
- **Reason for adjustment**, if the analyst changed the rating.
- **Analyst suggestions & conditions**: controls or mitigations the analyst recommends. **Read these carefully.** The committee often turns them into approval conditions.

### Risk Overview (read-only for you)

- Seven category scores: Customer, Product, Geography, Transaction, Channel, Third Party, Fraud.
- **Inherent risk:** the risk *before* controls.
- **Residual risk:** the risk *after* the analyst's controls.

Ratings: LOW (0–25), MEDIUM (26–50), HIGH (51–75), CRITICAL (76–100).

### Similar past cases

Up to 3 of **your own** earlier requests that look similar, with their ratings and decisions. Click one to open it.

### Regulatory evidence

The regulations the system found relevant (document, page, authority). Click **View evidence** to read the passage, and use **Previous / Next** or the ← / → arrow keys to move through them.

### Track your Progress page

- The stepper shows each step's time, and the SLA panel shows how much of the 48 hours has been used.
- While the request is with you after a deferral, the SLA shows **"Paused (with owner)"**.

## 8. When the committee decides

You get a notification, and the request moves to **Done**. Click **View outcome →**.

| Decision | Notification you get | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| **Approved** | *"Request CR-… approved — The committee approved your change request. The workflow is complete."* | You can proceed. The request is closed |
| **Approved with conditions** | *"Request CR-… approved with conditions — … Submit evidence for each condition."* plus one *"Condition opened…"* notification per condition | You can proceed **once each condition is evidenced and verified**. See [section 9](#9-approval-conditions-submitting-evidence) |
| **Deferred** | *"Request CR-… deferred — The committee deferred your request. Review the rationale and resubmit when ready."* | Either it is back with you ([section 10](#10-when-your-request-is-returned-for-rework)), or it is with the analyst and you simply wait |
| **Rejected** | *"Request CR-… rejected — The committee rejected your change request."* | Do not proceed. The request is closed |

The **Outcome** view shows:

- the title (Approved / Approved with conditions / Rejected)
- *"Final rating: X (system residual NN.N, methodology vN)"*
- *"Committee rationale (name): …"*
- *"Decided on revision N after M deferral(s)."*, if it was deferred before

## 9. Approval conditions: submitting evidence

If the decision was **Approve with conditions**, the Outcome view shows an **"Approval conditions"** box.

- Header: *"The Business Owner evidences each condition; the Risk Analyst verifies it."*
- Counter: *"{verified} of {total} verified · {n} overdue"*

Each condition shows:

- its description
- a status pill: **Open** / **Evidence submitted** / **Verified**
- *"Due {date}"* (default 30 days after the decision)
- a red **Overdue** badge if the due date has passed

**Your action, for each Open condition:**

1. Type into *"Describe the evidence that this condition is met..."*. For example: "Enhanced transaction monitoring rule TM-17 enabled in production on 12 Oct; screenshot in ticket CHG-4411."
2. Click **Submit evidence**.

> Evidence is a **text note**. There is no file upload, so reference where the proof lives (ticket number, document link, etc.).

**What happens:**

- The status becomes **Evidence submitted**.
- The analyst is notified: *"Evidence submitted for CR-…"*.
- An audit event `CONDITION_EVIDENCE_SUBMITTED` is recorded.

**Then the Risk Analyst either:**

- **Verifies** it: the status becomes **Verified**, and you get *"Condition verified on CR-…"*.
- **Sends it back**: the status returns to **Open** with *"Analyst feedback: …"*, and you get *"Evidence rejected on CR-… Please resubmit."* Fix it and submit again.

When **every** condition is verified:

- The status becomes **"Approved — conditions met"** and the request is **closed**.
- You get *"All conditions met on CR-…"* and a feedback request.

> **What others see now:**
>
> - **Risk Analyst:** the condition with your evidence, plus **Verify** and **Send back** buttons.
> - **Risk Committee:** the conditions list, read-only, and a notification when all are met.

If a condition passes its due date while still unverified, you get *"Condition overdue on CR-…"*.

## 10. When your request is returned for rework

If the committee **defers to the Business Owner**:

- The status becomes **"Returned for rework"** and the request goes back to the **Request** stage.
- Your intake becomes **editable again**: the Intake panel and Save buttons come back.
- A banner shows:
  - *"The Risk Committee returned this request to you for rework (name)."*
  - *"Committee's reasons: …"*
  - *"Update the inputs or controls and resubmit. The SLA clock is paused while the request is with you."*

**What to do:**

1. Read the committee's reasons.
2. Edit the relevant tabs and **Save** each one.
3. Go to **Preview**, then click **Submit for Risk Analyst review** again.

**Why the SLA pauses:** the bank's 48-hour promise covers time the bank spends on your request. Time spent waiting for you is not counted against the risk team.

The request starts a new **revision** (Revision 2, 3, …). The analyst runs a fresh assessment, and the history of earlier revisions is kept in the audit trail.

> **What others see now:**
>
> - **Risk Analyst:** the request **disappears** from their queue while it's with you. They get no notification until you resubmit.
> - **Risk Committee:** the request with status *"Returned for rework"*.

If the committee **defers to the Risk Analyst** instead, the status becomes **"Deferred for reassessment"**. You don't need to do anything; it goes back to the analyst and then to the committee again.

## 11. Rating your experience (feedback)

Once your request is **closed** (Approved, Rejected, or Approved with all conditions met), you can rate it for **5 days** by default. An Admin can change this window.

**Where:**

- **Dashboard banner:** *"Rate your experience (n request(s) closed)"*. Each closed request has a **Rate** button.
- **On the request page:** a card titled *"How did this request go?"*.

**The form:**

| Part | Options |
|---|---|
| Stars (required) | 1 Very poor · 2 Poor · 3 Okay · 4 Good · 5 Excellent |
| Tags (optional) | Accurate risk rating · Clear explanation · Fast turnaround · Easy to use · Rating felt too high · Rating felt too low · Too slow · Unclear next steps |
| Comment (optional) | *"Anything we should improve?"* |

Click **Send feedback**, or **Later** in the popup. You can rate each request **once**.

**Why it matters:** Admins review low ratings first, and the results guide improvements to the process.

## 14. FAQ and troubleshooting

**I submitted but want to change something.**
You can't, because the inputs are locked. Ask the analyst or committee. They can defer it back to you, or an Admin can edit it.

**"Submit for Risk Analyst review" is greyed out.**
Some required field isn't **saved**. The helper text lists which ones.

**Extract fields is greyed out.**
Upload a BRD with readable text first. Scanned images don't work.

**"Failed to extract BRD. Try a .txt file or check GEMINI_API_KEY."**
The AI and the rule-based parser both found nothing. Fill the tabs manually.

**I can't see the risk score.**
It's released only after the analyst submits their review.

**Will changing my name in Settings hide my requests?**
No. Your requests move to your new name automatically. The exception is when another account has the same old name; then ask an Admin.

**Can I delete a draft?**
There is no delete button. Leave it as a draft.
